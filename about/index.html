About JusB Apps FZE

Useful software.
Thoughtfully focused.

JusB Apps FZE is a software developer and publisher based in the United Arab Emirates. We create apps for everyday personal and business tasks.

Built around the way
people actually work.

Spending, assets and lead information each need a clear home. Our product family brings these tasks into focused applications, with useful records and straightforward ways to review them.

JusSpend focuses on spending and financial records. JusAssets focuses on asset information and supporting documents. JusLeads focuses on prospects, contact details and follow-ups. JusInventory is our inventory product in development.

We favour a consistent experience across the family while keeping each product’s purpose and records separate. Feedback and support enquiries help us understand where the experience needs to improve.

01

Make the task clear.

Give everyday information a useful structure, with labels and controls that help people complete the job.

02

Respect the record.

Use a local-first approach to core working data, with product-specific privacy information and user-controlled exports where supported.

03

Stay approachable.

Provide a direct contact for product questions, support and feedback, and clear terms for using our software.
