Useful information

Before you contact us.

Where can I get the apps?

Visit the product pages for current information and verified links. Contact us for platform and release availability. JusLeads also has a web app.

Where are my records stored?

Core records are generally stored locally on your device or browser unless a feature clearly states otherwise. Read the app-specific privacy policy for details. JusB Apps FZE does not automatically receive a copy of those records.

What should I do before changing devices?

Check the export, backup and restore options available in your app and protect any exported files. Local records do not automatically move with an app-store purchase or entitlement. Contact support before resetting or removing the app if you are unsure.

How do I ask about a purchase?

Tell us the app, store and purchase issue. Where supported, use Restore Purchases with the original store account. Purchase entitlements and locally stored records are separate. Do not send card details.

Can I request a feature or report a problem?

Yes. Email admin@jusbapps.com with the app name, the task you are trying to complete and your suggestion.
