JusB Apps are designed around a local-first approach. Core user-created records are generally stored locally on your device unless a particular feature clearly states that an external service is being used.

JusB Apps FZE does not automatically receive a copy of locally stored user-created records.

Applications may provide export, import, backup or restore functions. These actions are initiated by you.

5. Camera, Images and On-device Processing

Where an app offers camera, image-import or optical-character-recognition features, access is used only when you choose the relevant feature. App-specific policies explain the image and text processing used by each product.

6. Local Notifications

Where an app offers reminders or alerts, it may request notification permission and schedule notifications locally on your device. Local notifications are not used for advertising or behavioural tracking.

7. Purchases and Entitlements

Where Premium functionality is offered, purchases may be processed through the applicable application store and approved entitlement-management services.

JusB Apps FZE does not receive or store your full payment-card details from application-store purchases.

8. Support

If you contact JusB Apps FZE for support, we may receive the information you choose to provide.

9. Deleting Your Data

Where supported, locally stored application data can be removed using controls within the application or by removing the application from your device.

10. Security and Your Responsibility

You are responsible for maintaining appropriate device security and protecting backups or exported files.

11. Data Retention

Locally stored application data remains on your device until you delete it, reset the application, uninstall it or otherwise remove it.
