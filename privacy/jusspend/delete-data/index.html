JusSpend - Data deletion requests

Last updated: 01/11/2026

This page explains how users of JusSpend, published by JusB Apps FZE, can remove data stored on their device and request deletion of personal information held by the developer. JusSpend does not require a JusB Apps account to use its core features.

1. Delete locally stored app data

JusSpend is a local-first app. Core information, including spending, income and financial-tracking records, receipt images, extracted receipt information, categories and preferences, is held on your device or browser unless a feature clearly states otherwise. It is not automatically uploaded to JusB Apps FZE servers.

To remove a specific transaction, receipt or other saved record, use the available delete or remove controls in the app. To remove all local working data, use any available app reset or data-removal control, or your device's app-data removal controls, as supported by your platform. Removing the app may also remove its local records. Be sure to export anything you need before deletion, because local deletion can be permanent.

Copies you have exported or backed up, including exported financial records and backups, must be deleted separately from the places where you saved them. JusB Apps FZE cannot remotely erase records held only on your device.

2. Request deletion from JusB Apps FZE

  1. Send an email to admin@jusbapps.com.
  2. Use the subject JusSpend data deletion request.
  3. Describe the personal data you wish to have deleted. For support correspondence, identify the email address used. For a Premium purchase, say whether it was through Google Play and optionally provide the relevant order reference or anonymous app identifier, if available.
  4. We will review your request, verify it where necessary, delete information that we control where legally permitted, and explain any applicable retention requirement or service-provider process.

Do not send payment-card numbers, passwords or unrelated private records.

3. Data covered and retention

Deletion requests may include personal information you chose to send directly to JusB Apps FZE in support correspondence or enquiries, and purchase-related identifiers or entitlement information where we have the authority and technical ability to remove them.

Google Play processes purchases and may retain payment or order records. RevenueCat may process purchase, device and entitlement information used to grant or restore Premium access. Some records may be retained by these providers or the developer where required for legal, financial, fraud-prevention, dispute or operational reasons. We will explain any relevant limits and direct you to the appropriate provider when applicable.

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it through the available app or device controls. Exported copies remain wherever you saved them until you delete those copies. Information we hold directly is retained only while needed for the relevant purpose or a lawful retention requirement.

Deleting purchase-related entitlement information can affect access to Premium or restoration. A data-deletion request does not automatically cancel an active Google Play subscription; subscriptions must be managed separately through Google Play.
